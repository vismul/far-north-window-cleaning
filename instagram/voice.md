## My positions

1. Salt is doing damage right now and you cannot see it yet. Left through a wet
   season it bonds to the glass, and etched glass is a replacement job, not a
   cleaning job. That costs far more than a clean.
2. The pane is not what makes a window look bad. It is the grit in the track,
   the salt on the screen and the mould in the corner of the sill. Cleaning only
   the glass is half a job.
3. Ladders leaning on your gutters are not how upstairs glass should be done.
   Water-fed pole from the ground, every time.
4. You should have the number before the work starts, not a surprise afterwards.
   Priced on the spot, no call-out fee.
5. Not every house needs a three month cycle. Beachside yes, Redlynch probably
   not, and I will tell you which one you are.
