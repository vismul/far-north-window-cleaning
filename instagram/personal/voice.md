## My positions

1. There's a third option at 18 that nobody mentions. It's not uni and it's not
   an apprenticeship. It's starting something small on a Saturday.
2. Posting the bad numbers is the whole point. An account that only shows wins
   is an ad, and nobody follows an ad.
3. Buying gear feels like starting a business and it isn't. Knocking is.
4. Saying your price out loud and then shutting up is the hardest skill in this
   and it has nothing to do with cleaning.
5. Being 18 is the objection and the hook at the same time.
