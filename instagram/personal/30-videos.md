`instagram/personal/voice.md`. Every hook is scored against the 26 formulas,
every script is timed at 165 wpm, every script passes the `/ig-human` panel.

Video 1 uses your hook exactly as you wrote it. It scored **74.4 STRONG**, so
there was no reason to touch it.

Each video below carries **2 to 3 alternate hooks**, scored, off different
formulas. Shoot the one that matches the day you actually had.

## The b-roll you need on file

Most of these scripts reuse the same shots. Record these once, keep them in an
album, and most videos become a 5 minute edit:

- Pole going up a pane, from the ground, wide
- Water sheeting down glass, close
- Squeegee pass, clean edge, close
- Etched or hazy pane in raking light, macro
- Track grit being vacuumed, macro
- Screens out on the grass, being washed
- Loading and unloading gear in the dark
- Walking up a driveway, back of your head
- A gate opening, from your eye line
- Phone showing a booking, an invoice, a bank balance
- Notebook with the week's jobs written out
- Sunrise, and the drive to the first job
- You setting the tripod up in someone's yard
- The full kit laid out on a tarp
- Van or bike with the logo, wide, driving off
